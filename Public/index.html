Key Technological Innovations

Moving beyond archaic physical complaint boxes into an intelligent, proactive campus governance ecosystem.

🤖

Real-Time AI Urgency & Triage

Natural Language processing analyzes grievance text live as students type. Crisis terms (ragging, mental distress, electrical hazard) auto-trigger P0 Critical priority with a mandatory 4-hour SLA window.

🛡️

Zero-Knowledge Whistleblower

Allows victims of harassment, grading bias, or ragging to report with 100% anonymity. Generates an encrypted ANON-XXXX-XX token so students can track progress and communicate without fear.

⏱️

4-Tier Automated SLA Escalation

Prevents complaints from being buried. If a technician does not act within 24h, the ticket auto-escalates to the HOD/Warden (48h), Dean (72h), and ultimately the College Director with audit flags.

🤝

Two-Way Resolution Verification

Eliminates fake ticket closures. When administration marks work complete, it enters "Pending Student Verification". The student must verify and rate the fix, or dispute and reopen with 1-click.

🎙️

Multi-Modal Voice & Geo Evidence

Includes in-browser voice memo recording with live waveform frequency capture, camera image uploads, and campus location tagging across hostels and labs.

📊

Campus Hotspot Heatmap & Deduplication

Semantic algorithms detect recurring complaints (e.g. 50 students reporting the same broken water cooler) and aggregate them into single upvotable community issues with failure hotspot analytics.
